## Recommended Essential assessment criteria

These are the things the CV assessment should genuinely discriminate on.

### E1. Applied engineering problem solving
Essential | Highest assessment priority

Demonstrates the ability to understand a technical problem, develop or evaluate
solutions, and apply engineering reasoning to a practical challenge.

- **Strong**
  - Multiple examples where the candidate identifies the problem, explains their
    contribution or approach, and provides an outcome.
  - Evidence of iteration, investigation, trade-offs or learning.
- **Evidence**
  - One clear example of tackling an engineering or technical problem and applying
    knowledge to reach a solution.
- **Partial**
  - Participation in an engineering project is stated, but the candidate's
    contribution or reasoning is unclear.
- **None**
  - No applied problem-solving evidence located.

Why: The specification explicitly asks for passion for design and problem solving
and application of skills to practical engineering challenges.

### E2. Design and making
Essential

Demonstrates genuine engagement with designing, developing, building or improving
something. This can be physical or digital. Intentionally avoids making CAD a
requirement here.

- **Strong**
  - Multiple examples of developing a design from problem/idea through iteration,
    manufacture, implementation or testing.
- **Evidence**
  - One clear example of designing, building or developing something.
- **Partial**
  - Candidate says they enjoy design or lists design tools but gives no applied
    example.
- **None**
  - No design/development activity identified.

Why: Reflects the role's emphasis on design while avoiding turning the desirable
CAD criterion into an accidental prerequisite. The placement itself teaches
CATIA 3DX.

### E3. Technical curiosity and learning
Essential

Demonstrates active learning, investigation or experimentation beyond simply
completing assigned tasks.

- **Strong**
  - Multiple examples of independently learning something, investigating
    alternatives, experimenting or applying new knowledge.
- **Evidence**
  - One clear example of independently learning or exploring a technical subject.
- **Partial**
  - Claims curiosity, enthusiasm or willingness to learn without supporting evidence.
- **None**
  - Nothing identified.

Why: The specification explicitly says curiosity and technical potential matter
more than prior motorsport interest.

### E4. Values evidence
Essential

Provides concrete examples consistent with Accountable, Brave, Competitive and
Selfless behaviours. Evidence for all four is NOT required from a CV; assess the
strength of behavioural evidence available.

- **Strong**
  - Several concrete examples demonstrating different relevant behaviours, with
    the candidate's contribution clear.
- **Evidence**
  - At least one convincing example of behaviour aligned with the stated values.
- **Partial**
  - Values or characteristics are asserted but not demonstrated.
- **None**
  - No relevant behavioural evidence.

Why: The requirement to demonstrate how experience reflects Accountable, Brave,
Competitive and Selfless is explicitly essential in the specification.

## Desirable criteria

### D1. Applied CAD / 3D design experience
Desirable

Has applied CAD, 3D modelling or another design tool to produce or develop
engineering designs.

- **Strong:** recurring/substantial applied use across multiple projects, including
  design development or iteration.
- **Evidence:** one clear project involving applied CAD/3D modelling.
- **Partial:** software listed or proficiency self-declared without an applied example.
- **None:** no evidence.

Note: Do not require CATIA specifically. The specification asks for experience with
CAD, 3D modelling or other design tools and states CATIA 3DX proficiency is
something candidates will learn on placement.

### D2. Computational tools and process improvement
Desirable

Demonstrates application of programming, scripting, AI or other digital tools to
solve a problem, automate work or improve an engineering process.

- **Strong:** multiple applied examples and tangible improvement/outcome.
- **Evidence:** one clear applied example.
- **Partial:** language/tool listed without an application.
- **None:** no evidence.

Note: Combines the programming, scripting, practical AI and engineering-process-
improvement elements already grouped together in the desirable criteria.

### D3. Team contribution and collaboration
Desirable

Demonstrates effective contribution within a team, including working with others
towards a shared technical or practical outcome.

- **Strong:** clear personal contribution plus evidence of working across roles/
  disciplines or helping the wider team succeed.
- **Evidence:** clear contribution to a collaborative project.
- **Partial:** team membership stated without explaining their contribution.
- **None:** nothing located.

Note: Maps to the stated desirable requirement for an adaptable, proactive and
collaborative approach and effective team contribution.

### D4. Analytical rigour and attention to detail
Desirable

Demonstrates a methodical approach to analysing, testing, validating or improving
technical work.

- **Strong:** several concrete examples involving analysis/validation, with
  conclusions or resulting decisions.
- **Evidence:** one clear analytical or validation example.
- **Partial:** self-description such as "analytical" or "attention to detail"
  without an example.
- **None:** nothing located.

Note: Comes directly from the desirable criterion covering attention to detail and
an analytical and inquisitive approach.

### D5. Resilience, adaptability and improvement
Desirable

Demonstrates effective response to setbacks, changing requirements, unsuccessful
approaches or feedback, and subsequent learning or improvement.

- **Strong:** specific examples showing challenge/change, response and subsequent
  improvement.
- **Evidence:** one credible example.
- **Partial:** candidate describes themselves as resilient/adaptable without evidence.
- **None:** nothing located.

Note: Combines the desirable requirements around adaptability, determination,
resilience and continuous improvement.
